🐎 Don't Forget
Sneak Attack 1d6 — once per turn, with a Finesse or Ranged weapon, and you need advantage or an ally within 5 ft of the target.
Charge (Centaur): move 30 ft straight at a target, hit it in melee, then take a bonus action hoof attack — 1d6+3.
Hooves replace your normal unarmed strike: 1d6 + STR bludgeoning.
Vex on both swords and bow: a hit gives you advantage on your next attack against that target — which also turns on Sneak Attack.
Speed 40 ft, but Equine Build makes any hands-and-feet climb cost 4 extra feet per foot. Look for stairs.
Expertise: Stealth +8 and Intimidation +5. Cunning Action does not arrive until level 2 — hiding still costs your action.
