🏹 Don't Forget
Hunter's Mark is always prepared and you get 2 free casts per long rest. Open with it — it is +1d6 on every hit.
Vex (Shortsword): hit a creature and you have advantage on your next attack against it.
Nick (Scimitar): the off-hand attack rides along with the Attack action — it does not cost your Bonus Action.
Natural Resilience: resistance to acid and poison, and advantage on saves against being poisoned.
Amorphous: you squeeze through a 1-inch gap with nothing worn or carried, and have advantage on grapple checks.
Hold Breath 1 hour, Darkvision 60 ft, and Stealth +6 — you are the scout.
Magic Initiate (Wizard) gives you two cantrips plus one free level-1 casting per long rest.
