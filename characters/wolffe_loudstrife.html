🔫 Don't Forget
Quick Draw gives you Advantage on Initiative — the Initiative +3 pill does not show it, so roll with advantage.
Archery is already in the numbers: the Revolver's +7 includes the +2 Fighting Style bonus. Melee weapons do not get it.
Slow (Revolver): a hit reduces the target's Speed by 10 ft until the start of your next turn.
The Revolver has the Reload property — track your bullets, and reloading costs an action.
Double Draw: you can draw or stow two one-handed weapons where others manage one.
